Savanah

3D savanna and animal pages

Savanna Endless painted savanna with trees, water, birds and 5 moods (day to night). Animal night demo One animal, full screen, under the stars. Drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap the animal and it looks at you. Phone remote Control the animal night demo from your phone, with a live view. Open the demo on the big screen first. Animals sandbox All 11 animal models side by side, with every animation.

The phone remote needs the home server (serve.py) or its Cloudflare link: on this GitHub site the other pages work, but the remote cannot reach the demo.
